It has become obvious that building an effective marketing lies most in combining several channels VS using one channel. Social media, search engines, and email all function better when they are part of a connected strategy that builds trust and brings people back to your business. According to Statista, businesses that rely on multiple channels notice up to 250% more engagement than those that use only one .
